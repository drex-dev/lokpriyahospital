Who we are

Built from a dream of world-class care close to home.

Lokpriya Hospital was conceived as a state-of-the-art healthcare destination for patients from Western U.P. It is designed as a comprehensive medical facility with a Core Hospital and several Centres of Excellence (CoEs), plus support services and accommodation.

We started with about 50 beds and have expanded to 195 beds, with plans to grow further. At Lokpriya, your health is our highest priority — backed by advanced medical technology, expert doctors and round-the-clock emergency care.
