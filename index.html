About Lokpriya

A state-of-the-art healthcare destination for Western U.P.

Lokpriya Hospital is a comprehensive medical facility with a Core Hospital and several Centres of Excellence, supported by allied services and accommodation. We began with about 50 beds and have grown to 195 — and we're still expanding.
